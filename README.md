## Abstract

Dropout rates are a concern for higher education institutions due to their implications for both student success and institutional performance. This study presents the use of machine learning techniques to forecast student dropout based on academic, demographic, socioeconomic, and macroeconomic data collected from a Portuguese university. Two machine learning algorithms—Logistic Regression and Support Vector Machine (SVM)—were trained and compared using various performance measures, with particular interest in recall and F1-score. The best-performing model achieved an F1-score of 0.7876 and a recall of 0.8476, demonstrating great ability in identifying at-risk students. The results enhance the potential of data-driven approaches to support early intervention strategies and improve student retention in higher education.
